SpendWise - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 28 May 2026

At SpendWise, we value your privacy. This policy explains how your data is collected, used, and protected while using the SpendWise mobile application.

1. Data Collected and Purpose of Use

  • Financial Data: Expense and debt records you enter into the app are stored entirely on your device (locally). These records are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Camera Access: Required for scanning receipts to detect amounts (OCR feature). Captured images are processed instantly and not stored.
  • Biometric Data: Fingerprint or face recognition may be used for the in‑app lock feature. SpendWise does not access your actual biometric data; verification is securely handled by the Android operating system.

2. Third‑Party Services

  • Google Play Services: For app infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob: To display ads within the app. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to deliver personalized ads.
  • Google ML Kit: For text recognition (OCR) performed on‑device using Google’s libraries.

3. Data Security

Your data is stored in a secure local database on your device. When you uninstall the app, all financial data stored on your device will also be deleted. Users are responsible for backing up their own data.

4. Children’s Privacy

SpendWise does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. It is a financial management tool suitable for all age groups; however, ad content is managed by third parties.

5. Permissions

To function properly, the app requests the following permissions:

  • CAMERA: For receipt scanning.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: For budget limit alerts.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: For app lock functionality.
